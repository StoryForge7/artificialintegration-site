FAQ

Questions.

Which roles does it suit?

Any construction or engineering desk: commercial and QS, project and site management, design, engineering, M&E and civils.

Where do the signals come from?

Contracts Finder and Find a Tender, the UK's public procurement notices, checked every hour. Workstation keeps construction works and architecture, engineering and surveying awards that match your market.

Is it only public-sector work?

The awards are public, but the winners are private contractors and consultancies. A firm that wins public work is usually hiring across its whole pipeline.

Does it work with our CRM?

Yes. Import from Bullhorn, Vincere, Recruit CRM, Zoho Recruit, Loxo or Manatal, or start from the spreadsheet your consultants already use. Nothing is changed in your CRM.
