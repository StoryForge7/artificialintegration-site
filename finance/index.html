FAQ

Questions.

Does it handle perm and interim?

Both. Deals track perm fees as salary × fee % and contract or interim fees as margin × days, all in one funnel per consultant.

Which areas of finance does it suit?

Accountancy and audit practice, tax, advisory, banking, insurance, pensions and in-house finance teams.

Where do the signals come from?

Contracts Finder and Find a Tender, checked every hour. Workstation keeps accounting, audit and tax awards and financial and insurance services awards that match your market.

Does it work with our CRM?

Yes. Import from Bullhorn, Vincere, Recruit CRM, Zoho Recruit, Loxo or Manatal, or start from the spreadsheet your consultants already use. Nothing is changed in your CRM.
