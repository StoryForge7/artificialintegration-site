FAQ

Questions.

Does it work for in-house as well as private practice?

Yes. Mapping, call lists, CVs and specs work for any legal desk. Hiring signals cover firms winning public legal work.

How do you keep candidates confidential?

CVs come out with the candidate's contact details removed. Exclusions and do-not-contact lists stop a CV reaching the wrong firm, and every send is recorded.

Where do the signals come from?

Contracts Finder and Find a Tender, checked every hour. Workstation keeps legal services awards and matches them to your market.

Does it work with our CRM?

Yes. Import from Bullhorn, Vincere, Recruit CRM, Zoho Recruit, Loxo or Manatal, or start from the spreadsheet your consultants already use. Nothing is changed in your CRM.
