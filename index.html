FAQ

Questions.

Does Workstation replace my consultants?

No. It does the preparation — the call list, the research, the first draft — so consultants spend their time on the phone and in meetings. Every message is theirs to edit before it goes.

Do we need Lusha?

Yes. Workstation connects to your agency's existing Lusha account. Owners set the key once and choose a daily limit per person; usage is shown per consultant.

Does it replace our CRM?

It can sit alongside it. Many agencies start by importing the spreadsheet their consultants already work from, and Workstation becomes the daily workspace on top.

Which sectors is it for?

Any white-collar recruitment desk. Insights covers twelve industries, including construction, engineering, legal, finance, IT, healthcare and education. You choose yours in Settings, and the call list and hiring signals follow. See the pages for construction, legal and finance recruiters.

Will emails come from us?

Yes. Spec emails and outreach send from each consultant's own Gmail or Outlook, so replies arrive in the inbox they already use.

How long does set-up take?

Usually a single session. We create your agency, invite your team, import your spreadsheet and set up your market profile with you, so Insights is relevant from day one.

Can we trial it first?

Yes. We run pilots with an agreed goal and dates, and review placements, fees and usage with you at the end.

How is it priced?

Per seat, per month. We'll walk you through it on the demo once we know the size of your team.
