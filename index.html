FAQ

Questions.

What is an AI receptionist?

Software that answers your phone, holds a natural conversation, collects the caller's details, and sends you a summary. It handles the calls you would otherwise miss.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Your customers keep ringing the number they already know. We give your receptionist its own line behind the scenes, and you forward calls to it — when you're busy, when you don't answer, or always. Most UK mobile and landline networks support this with a short code.

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and is trained on your business. If a caller asks, it says it's an AI assistant — we don't configure it to claim otherwise.

What happens with emergencies?

You define what counts as urgent. Those calls can be transferred live to you or a team member, and are flagged in the summary you receive.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few working days. We build the receptionist after one short call with you; UK phone lines need a quick regulatory check before they're issued. Then you make a test call and switch forwarding on.

Can I control when it answers?

Yes, two ways. Forwarding decides which calls reach it — only when you don't pick up, only when you're busy, or all calls — and you can switch it on or off from your phone. The receptionist also has working hours you set.

Are calls recorded? What about GDPR?

Calls are transcribed and can be recorded. Callers are told at the start of the call. Data is processed under UK GDPR and we sign a data processing agreement with every customer. See our privacy policy.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly rolling, cancel any time.
