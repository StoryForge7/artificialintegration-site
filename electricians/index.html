What it asks

The questions you'd ask.

Set up with the details you need to price a job or decide if it can't wait, so you ring back ready.

01

The fault or the job

Tripping RCD, dead sockets, rewire, consumer unit, EV charger, EICR.

02

Is it safe?

Burning smell, scorch marks, sparking, no power. You choose what gets put straight through.

03

The property

House or flat, size, age of the board, who owns it.

04

Who's calling

Homeowner, landlord, letting agent, or a builder you work with.

05

Where

Postcode, so you can tell straight away whether it's in your area.

06

When

Today, this week, or before a deadline like a sale or a new tenant.

Emergencies and regulars

The calls that matter get through.

You set the rules. Emergencies and the builders and agents you work with can be put straight through to you. Everything else is captured and summarised.

  • No power or burning smell? Put through to you live
  • New quotes captured with the details you need
  • Landlords texted a link to book their EICR
  • After-hours calls answered, not sent to voicemail
